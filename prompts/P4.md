## PART 0 — WHY THIS PAGE IS DIFFERENT

Visa Services is **not** built using the `ServicePageTemplate` from Turn 2. It is a purpose-built, standalone page with its own layout. Three reasons:

1. It is the highest-trust page on the site. A visitor is considering handing over passport documents and personal information to a company they've just discovered. The page has to earn that trust at every scroll position.

2. It carries **legal compliance requirements** that no other page has. The wording rules are non-negotiable and must be woven into the design — not buried in a disclaimer.

3. It has structural content no other page needs: a process walkthrough, destination-specific cards with visa type information, and a transparency statement that must feel premium rather than defensive.

**File:** `app/visa-services/page.jsx`
**Component folder:** `components/visa/` — all sub-components for this page live here.

### Section 2 — Process: How We Help

**Background:** `--color-white`

**Section padding:** `--section-padding-y` top and bottom (96px each).

**Section header — left-aligned, within max-width container:**

- Subtext below: "A straightforward process from first enquiry to visa submission." — `--font-body`, 16px, `--color-stone`, margin-top 12px

**48px gap below the header.**

**Process steps layout:**

Four steps displayed in a horizontal row on desktop, stacked vertically on mobile.

**Why numbered here:** A visa application is a genuine sequential process — step 1 must happen before step 2. Numbered steps are therefore structurally correct here, not decorative. The visual treatment makes them editorial rather than generic.

**Each step structure (top to bottom):**

1. **Step number** — `--font-display`, 64px, weight 300, `--color-gold` at 18% opacity. Displayed as a large background numeral behind the step content — not a circle badge. Positioned absolutely behind the step text, creating depth without dominating.

2. **Step title** — `--font-body`, 16px, weight 600, `--color-ink`. Positioned in front of the numeral.

3. **Step description** — `--font-body`, 14px, `--color-stone`, line-height 1.65, max-width 220px.

**Connector between steps (desktop only):** A thin `1px` horizontal line in `--color-border` runs between step boxes, connecting them visually. The line should not have arrowheads — it is a connective element, not a directional indicator.

**The four steps:**

| Step | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Get in touch | Tell us your destination, nationality and travel dates. We'll let you know what's required for your specific situation. |
| 2 | Document preparation | We provide a full checklist of required documents and review everything before submission to reduce the risk of avoidable errors. |
| 3 | Application handled | We prepare and organise your application. Where direct submission is available, we handle it on your behalf. |
| 4 | Stay informed | We keep you updated throughout the process and notify you as soon as a decision is received. |

**Below the steps row — a single full-width note in `--color-stone`, `--font-body`, 14px, centered, italic:**

*"Visa decisions are made solely by the relevant immigration or consular authority. We cannot influence or guarantee outcomes."*

This note appears directly beneath the steps, clearly associated with the process. It is not hidden — it is part of the process explanation. This is the legally important wording, handled with design care rather than small-print defensiveness.

## PART 3 — WORDING COMPLIANCE RULES

The agent must treat these as hard constraints, not suggestions. Flag any variation.

**Never use on this page or anywhere in the codebase:**
- "Guaranteed visa"
- "100% approval rate"
- "We guarantee your visa"
- "Visa approval guaranteed"
- "Success rate" followed by any percentage
- Any phrasing that implies the outcome is within the agency's control

**Always use:**
- "Professional visa application assistance"
- "Document guidance and preparation"
- "We assist with your application"
- "The final decision rests with the relevant immigration or consular authority"
- "We cannot guarantee outcomes"

**These phrases must appear on the page in at least two places:** once in the process section (as the italic note beneath the steps) and once in the Transparency Statement section. The agent does not consolidate them into one location.

## PART 4 — DESIGN AND ANIMATION DETAILS

### Color application across sections

| Section | Background |
|---|---|
| Hero | Image + dark gradient overlay |
| How We Help | `--color-white` |
| Visa Destinations | `--color-ice` |
| Why Professional Assistance | `--color-white` |
| FAQ | `--color-ice` |
| Transparency Statement | `--color-abyss` |
| Enquiry CTA | `--color-deep-navy` |

The alternating white / ice / white / ice / dark / dark rhythm creates natural section breaks without hard lines or borders between sections.

### ScrollTrigger behavior — varied, not uniform

- **Section 2 (Process steps):** Steps reveal sequentially left to right, 0.15s stagger. The large background numerals fade in slightly before the text content of each step — a subtle layered effect.
- **Section 3 (Destinations grid):** Cards stagger in at 0.08s per card. Four cards per row, so the stagger completes quickly and doesn't feel slow.
- **Section 4 (Why professional assistance):** Left column text block reveals as one unit. Right column benefit rows stagger in from top to bottom at 0.12s intervals, beginning 0.2s after the left column starts.
- **Section 5 (FAQ):** The accordion container fades in as a single block. No per-item animation — staggering accordion items feels fidgety.
- **Section 6 (Transparency Statement):** The horizontal rules draw in from center outward (scale X from 0 to 1, transform-origin center). Then the content fades in. This makes the section feel deliberately placed, not incidental.
- **Section 7 (CTA):** Single fade-in for the full content block together. No stagger.

### Active nav state

`/visa-services` is a top-level nav item in PillNav. When on this route, `activeHref` passed to PillNav matches the Visa Services item and displays the gold active indicator.
