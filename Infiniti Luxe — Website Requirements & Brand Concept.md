# 7. Visa Section

This is potentially a major lead generator.

## Your Journey Starts Here

> Visa assistance for destinations around the world.

### Popular Visa Destinations

- Schengen
- UK
- USA
- Canada
- Australia
- Japan
- Saudi Arabia
- UAE
- Turkey
- etc.

### Visa Wording

Be careful with wording.

Don't say:

> ❌ 100% Visa Approval

Don't say:

> ❌ Guaranteed Visa

Instead:

> **Professional visa application assistance and document guidance.**

> The final decision remains with the relevant immigration/consular authority.
